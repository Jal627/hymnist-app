User guide

Quick start

Hymnist shows song lyrics on a projector screen and follows along as your church sings. Here is the fastest way to a Sunday-ready setup:

  1. Build your setlist. In Today’s setlist, search for a song and tap Add to setlist.
  2. Open the projector screen. Tap Open projector, drag the new window onto the projector, and click it once for full screen.
  3. Start the music. Tap the microphone and let Hymnist follow the singing, or tap Next line yourself. You are always in control.
Everything you change is saved on your device automatically. Nothing is lost if you close the page.

The Operator screen

This is the control center, used by the person running the lyrics. The three tabs at the top switch between Operator, Musicians and Projector.

  1. 1Preview: exactly what the congregation will see.
  2. 2Next line (and Back, Blank, Prayer beside it). Blank hides the words; Prayer shows your prayer message.
  3. 3Open projector opens the congregation screen in its own window.
  4. 4Full screen hides everything except the preview, the buttons and the Listen panel.
  5. 5Microphone: tap to let Hymnist listen and advance the lyrics for you.
  6. 6Plan ahead (calendar): create setlists for upcoming services.
  7. 7Song library (books): find, edit and add songs.

Listen: hands-free lyrics

Hymnist listens to the singing, finds the matching line in your setlist, and moves the screen to it.

  1. 1Tap the microphone to start or stop listening. The first time, allow microphone access when your device asks.
  2. 2No microphone? Type words as if they were sung and Hymnist reacts the same way. This is also a great way to practice.
  3. 3What it heard is shown here, so you can see what the app is working with.
  4. 4Match meter: the fuller the bar, the more confident the match.
It only matches against your setlist, so songs you did not add cannot pop up by mistake. The Next line and Back buttons always override the microphone.

If speech recognition is unreliable on a device, open Settings → Speech recognition and try Always on-device. It is slower, but it never sends audio anywhere.

Building a setlist

The Today’s setlist card is the list of songs for this service.

  1. 1Search for a song by title. Small typos are fine. Press Enter to add the first result.
  2. 2Add to setlist adds the song to the end of the list.
  3. 3Use the arrows to reorder songs and × to remove one from the setlist (it stays in your library).

A 🎸 icon next to a title means the song has chords. A ⚠ needs lyrics tag means it has no lyrics yet, so add some in the library before using it.

Song library and adding songs

Tap the books icon to open every song you have.

  1. 1Add a song (the + button).
  2. 2Search all songs.
  3. 3Edit changes the title, lyrics or chords. Add lyrics appears for songs that have none.
  4. 4Add puts the song in the setlist you are working on.

Adding a song

  1. 1Type the title.
  2. 2Paste the lyrics, one line per row. To add chords, put them in square brackets right before the word where the chord changes, like [G]Amazing [C]grace.
  3. 3Tap Save song. It is added to the library and to your current setlist.
Only add lyrics your church is licensed to display. Hymnist does not provide or license songs. The built-in hymns are believed to be public domain in the United States.

Planning ahead

Tap the calendar icon to build setlists for upcoming services and pick the one you want to run.

  1. 1Songs: choose and reorder the songs in that setlist.
  2. 2Open: make it the active setlist on the Operator screen.
  3. 3Edit: rename it or change its date and time.
  4. 4Give a new setlist a name, plus an optional date and time.

A setlist with a date and time switches on by itself when its start time arrives, so Sunday morning is ready without anyone clicking. Leave the date blank for an always-available setlist, such as a quick list for rehearsal. Past services are kept under Show past setlists.

The projector screen

The projector screen shows only the lyrics, large, with the background you chose.

  1. Tap Open projector on the Operator screen. A new window opens.
  2. Drag that window onto the projector or second display.
  3. Click inside it once to make it full screen. Press Esc to leave full screen.

It follows the Operator screen automatically, including Blank and Prayer. If your browser blocks the pop-up, allow pop-ups for this page, or open the app in a second tab with #display added to the end of the address.

Other devices: the projector window normally needs to be on the same computer and browser as the operator. If Settings → Sync status says you are connected, other devices follow the operator automatically.

Full-screen operator mode

Tap the full screen button next to the main buttons to hide everything except what you need while leading.

  1. 1The Listen panel stays at the bottom so you can see what the app is hearing.
  2. 2Tap the same button again (it now shows the exit icon) to return.

Musicians view

Tap Musicians to see the current song with chords for your instrument. The highlighted line follows the Operator screen.

  1. 1Instrument: Guitar shows chord shapes, Piano / Keys shows the notes, Bass shows root notes, Vocals shows lyrics only.
  2. 2Transpose: the − and + buttons move the key down or up one step at a time. The number shows how far you have moved.
  3. 3Text size: A− and A+ make everything smaller or larger.
  4. 4Musician settings (gear): choose a font and a text size.

These choices are saved on each device, so every player can set the screen the way they like without changing anyone else’s.

Settings

Tap the gear at the top right of the Operator screen to open Settings.

  1. 1User guide: this guide.
  2. 2Lyrics font: pick the font for the projector screen. A preview is shown below it.
  3. 3Projector lyrics size: make the words larger or smaller.
  4. 4Background: choose a preset, any custom color, or your own picture.
  5. 5Prayer message: the words shown when you tap Prayer.

Further down you will also find Speech recognition options, the Sync status, and the Legal notice & terms of use.

Some fonts load from the internet. If a device is offline it will show a similar built-in font instead.

Shortcuts and troubleshooting

Keyboard shortcuts (Operator screen)

  • → or Space Next line
  • ← Back
  • B Blank the screen
  • Enter in the search box adds the first match
  • Esc closes a window

Something not working?

“Mic error: not-allowed”
Microphone access is blocked. In a browser, tap the lock or settings icon next to the web address and set Microphone to Allow, then reload. In an installed app, open the phone’s Settings → Apps → Hymnist → Permissions and allow Microphone. If the app has no microphone option there, it was built without microphone access. Typing words into the Listen box still works.
The words don’t advance
Check that the song is in today’s setlist and has lyrics. Hymnist only matches songs in the setlist. You can always use Next line.
The projector window won’t open
Allow pop-ups for this page, or open a second tab with #display on the end of the address.
My songs and settings disappeared
They are stored in the browser on that device. Clearing the browser’s site data, or switching to a different browser or device, starts fresh.
Chords look wrong
The chords on the built-in hymns are simple, standard arrangements. Edit the song in the library to change them.
